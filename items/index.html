Held items and Mega Evolution

Equip a held item on a Pokémon’s Box page. The Shop and Bag group battle items by what they do, from attack boosts to weather. A Pokémon holding its Mega Stone Mega Evolves at the start of a battle, and Home, the floating pet and the menu bar show the Mega form. In the Shop, a second chip row shows one group at a time, and one search field finds any item, egg or TM by its name in any of the app’s languages. Every held item works in battles, and the Shop sells each one that has an effect. Gems cost 20M because they are used up, and items that hold their holder back, like the power items and the Iron Ball, cost 5M.

Moves and TMs

A Pokémon’s Box page lists its four moves, and you can swap one for a level-up, egg or TM move, including status moves like Swords Dance. Buy a TM once in the Shop and every Pokémon that can learn it may use it. Each slot is its own card, and ▲/▼ swap it with its neighbor to set the order. Move names follow the app language.

Status moves

Status moves have their own group in the move picker, each with a one-line effect. Every Gen 9 status move that works in a one-on-one battle is there, from Swords Dance and Thunder Wave to Protect, Substitute, Rest, Taunt, Baton Pass and Metronome. The few that do nothing in singles, like Whirlwind, Mean Look and Attract, are left out. In battle your Pokémon uses them by the trainers’ rules.

TMs with Power —

Attacks whose power comes from their effect, such as Gyro Ball, Electro Ball, Low Kick, Night Shade and Reversal, show Power — in the TM shop and the move picker. The Shop sells 14 of them as TMs at the lowest price. Every attacking move works in battle except Natural Gift and Fling.

Set the order

Each move slot is a card. ▲/▼ swap it with the slot above or below, so you can reorder without picking the moves again.

Egg moves with the Mirror Herb

A Pokémon’s attacking egg moves sit after its TMs, locked until you use a Mirror Herb on it. One herb unlocks them for good, along with the egg moves of what it evolved from. Buy it in the Shop, or press Use Mirror Herb in the move picker to buy and use one in a single step.

Every Pokémon has a profile.

Open a Pokémon in the Box for its level, gender, nature, ability, IVs, stats, held item and moves. Descriptions, abilities and moves follow your language, with English as the fallback.

Exp. Share

Attach an Exp. Share to one Pokémon in the Box. It gets 50% of the EXP your current Pokémon or egg earns, so a graduate keeps growing while you raise the next one. When a new egg sends your partner to the Box, an Exp. Share from your Bag goes on it. Move here on a Box page moves a copy from another holder, even with an empty Bag, and a holder in the Box evolves there when the next step needs only EXP, a level, friends or a type. A holder that can't use more EXP passes its copy to the closest Box line. A fully grown holder with no step left graduates by itself into your Pokédex. An Exp. Share left in the Bag goes on the Pokémon closest to its next step by itself, but never back on one you took it off yourself.

Only in PokeTokenBar-yp

Ability, nature, IVs and form

Your Pokémon can field what a gym trainer’s team does. The Mint opens a 5×5 chart and sets the nature you pick. The Ability Capsule and Patch switch abilities, and Bottle Caps raise IVs to 31. The Reveal Glass, DNA Splicers and Reins of Unity switch forms, and a form brings its own types, ability and stats to battle.
